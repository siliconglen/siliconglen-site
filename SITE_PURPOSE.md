# Site Purpose

This repository contains the source for siliconglen.com — a professional website for Craig Cockburn, promoting his consultancy services in Critical Thinking, Delivery, Business Agility, and AI.

The site exists to help prospective clients understand what Craig does, see evidence of his past work (through the Case Studies, Delivery, Business Agility, Critical Thinking, and AI pages), and contact him to discuss engaging his services (through the Work With Me pages).

For all locked design and content decisions governing how this site must look, read, and be structured, see `DESIGN_STANDARDS.md` in this repository root. Every change to this site must comply with that file.

For technical build and local development instructions, see `README.md`.
