# Site Purpose

This repository contains the source for siliconglen.com, the professional website of Craig Cockburn and his company, Siliconglen Ltd.

The site promotes Craig's services in agile coaching, programme and project delivery, and critical thinking, including Red Team Thinking® training delivered in partnership with Red Team Thinking®, and facilitated decision support.

The site's primary focus is genuine business-to-business (B2B) work contracted through Siliconglen Ltd, of any length, including:
- workshops and training;
- short, high-value engagements;
- speaking engagements;
- delivery and coaching engagements lasting many months or longer, for more complex work.

The site also reflects Craig's full career, including salaried roles, and other types of work it generates are welcome, but they are not what the site is designed for.

The site exists to help prospective clients understand what Craig does, see evidence of his past work (through the Case Studies, Delivery, Business Agility, Critical Thinking and AI pages), and contact him to engage his services (through the Work With Me pages).

For all locked design and content decisions governing how this site must look, read and be structured, see `DESIGN_STANDARDS.md` in this repository root. Every change to this site must comply with that file.

For technical build and local development instructions, see `README.md`.
