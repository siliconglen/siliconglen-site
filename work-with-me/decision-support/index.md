## A decision is only as sound as the thinking behind it

Difficult decisions often combine incomplete evidence, competing priorities and pressure to move. A plausible answer can arrive before the real question has been agreed.

I help leaders and teams frame the decision, distinguish evidence from interpretation and make uncertainty explicit. The decision remains yours; the work makes its basis clearer and more defensible.

## What we can cover

- The decision that actually needs to be made and who owns it.
- Assumptions, evidence gaps and alternative explanations.
- Options and explicit criteria for comparing them.
- Blind spots, dependencies and unintended consequences.
- What further evidence would materially change the choice.
- AI-generated analysis that needs human verification and challenge.

## How the work happens

### Frame
We define the question, desired outcome, constraints and decision point.

### Examine
We review the available evidence, interpretations, assumptions and options without pretending uncertainty can be eliminated.

### Challenge
We test the reasoning, look for missing perspectives and identify where confidence exceeds the evidence.

### Decide and act
We record the rationale, remaining uncertainty and practical next steps so the decision can move into delivery.

## What you leave with

Depending on the need, you may leave with a clearer decision statement, explicit criteria, an evidence and assumption map, tested options, prioritised risks and an actionable next-step plan. The goal is not a longer report; it is a decision people can understand and use.

## When this is useful

- The stakes are high or the choice is hard to reverse.
- Evidence is incomplete or senior people interpret it differently.
- The organisation is moving before success has been defined.
- A persuasive proposal has not received independent challenge.
- An AI-produced answer appears confident but has not been verified.

## Make the next decision clearer

Describe the choice, what is at stake and when it must be made. We can agree a proportionate next step.
