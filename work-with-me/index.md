## Start with the problem

You may need to make a difficult decision, challenge a strategy, recover a programme or improve how your organisation delivers. The starting point is the problem and the outcome you need—not a method imposed in advance.

My work brings together three connected capabilities. **Critical Thinking** tests assumptions and sharpens decisions. **Delivery** turns decisions into working outcomes. **Agile** supports adaptive delivery and organisational improvement. They can be used separately, but they are strongest when the work connects thinking to action and learning.

## Critical Thinking

Make the question clearer, examine the evidence and expose blind spots while there is still time to act.

- [Critical Thinking]({{ '/work-with-me/critical-thinking/' | relative_url }}) for practical challenge and clearer reasoning.
- [Red Team Thinking®]({{ '/work-with-me/red-team-thinking/' | relative_url }}) for structured, independent challenge to a strategy or plan.
- [Decision Support]({{ '/work-with-me/decision-support/' | relative_url }}) for a specific difficult decision.
- [Red Team Thinking Training]({{ '/work-with-me/red-team-thinking/' | relative_url }}) to help people become better thinkers.

## Delivery

Move beyond advice and plans to coordinate complex work and produce a result.

- [Programme & Project Delivery]({{ '/work-with-me/programme-delivery/' | relative_url }}) for complex programmes and change.

## Agile

Use feedback, evidence and adaptation to improve delivery rather than treating Agile as the whole answer.

- [Agile Coaching]({{ '/work-with-me/agile-coaching/' | relative_url }}) for adaptive delivery, organisational improvement and team leadership capability.

## How the work happens

We clarify the outcome, understand the situation and agree a proportionate piece of work. That may mean focused decision support, an independent challenge, hands-on delivery or a workshop. The work stays connected to evidence, practical action and what the organisation learns.

## What you leave with

The precise output follows the problem: a defensible decision, a stronger plan, a delivered outcome, clearer priorities or practical capability your people can keep using. The aim is useful change rather than activity for its own sake.

## Find the right starting point

You do not need to diagnose the service yourself. Describe the problem and what a good outcome would look like.
