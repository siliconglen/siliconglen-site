## Red Team Thinking: Applied critical thinking for leaders {#red-team-thinking-conference-talk}

A talk on decision-making in business: the issues and problems leaders face, and how additional tools adapted from the military into a business-friendly context, Red Team Thinking, can help. Covers a full list of the tools, with in-depth focus on a selection and their use for leaders, including within Product Management in agile. Given in Cluj, Romania, in September 2024, and also in India in 2025.

## Knowledge Canvas {#knowledge-canvas}

Knowledge Canvas is a mind-map alternative I developed for organising training and learning content into a single page, combining and extending concept maps, mind maps and Cornell notes with time-based structure and colour-coding to tell a story and help people learn and revise it. It's a digital-friendly format that also works alongside online video, with particular emphasis on people who prefer a clear structure.
{: .lede}

I developed it to make talks and conferences more inclusive for neurodivergent audiences: approximately one in seven people in the UK have a neurodifference, according to [Acas](https://www.acas.org.uk/neurodiversity-at-work). I released it under a [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

I first presented it at Agile Tour London in October 2020, then at Agile Lithuania on 7 September 2022, where the organisers invited me to present it early, ahead of the main conference, so other speakers could use the method while preparing their own talks. The approach has received strong positive feedback and has been used by a training company.

## Strategy Maps: connect your roadmaps to the bigger picture {#strategy-maps-agile-india-2022}

> "Most strategy dialogues end up with executives talking at cross-purposes because… nobody knows exactly what is meant by vision and strategy, and no two people ever quite agree on which topics belong where." — Geoffrey Moore, author and business adviser, Geoffrey Moore Consulting, *Escape Velocity*

All too often, strategy is a static PowerPoint that goes out of date and gives little indication of the "why of movement."

This talk shows how to form an agile strategy and visualise it in a complex landscape, connecting it to roadmaps, backlogs, Wardley mapping and the Strategy Cycle, drawing on work by Simon Wardley, Roman Pichler and Dave Snowden. It also uses a novel visualisation technique to help visualise the talk itself.
