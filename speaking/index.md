## Ideas that lead back to practical work

Speaking can give people a shared question, challenge a comfortable assumption and create a useful starting point for action. Craig's subjects connect critical thinking with delivery and organisational improvement, including how people can use AI without outsourcing judgement.

## Talks and video

No individual talks have been published in this site's talks collection yet. As material is added, this page will link to indexable pages containing the talk title, event, date, video URL, description, topics and an edited transcript or article where available.

Video will be hosted on YouTube and presented here using privacy-enhanced, performance-conscious embeds. The substantive context will remain available on the site rather than existing only on a social platform.

## What a session can cover

- Challenging assumptions and exposing blind spots.
- Making difficult decisions with incomplete evidence.
- Connecting good thinking to practical delivery.
- Agile as adaptive delivery and organisational improvement.
- Verification, false confidence and automation bias in AI.
- Facilitating a group around an important question or plan.

## How the work happens

We clarify the audience, setting, purpose and outcome before agreeing the format. A useful session is shaped around the real context rather than an invented list of past talks. Where facilitation is needed, we also define the question participants must work through and what should happen after the room disperses.

## What you leave with

A shared way to discuss the issue, questions worth pursuing and a clear connection between the ideas and the audience's work. The precise output depends on whether the need is a talk, a facilitated discussion or a practical workshop.

## When this is useful

- An event needs a speaker on critical thinking, delivery, Agile or AI.
- Leaders need a constructive challenge to established thinking.
- A group needs facilitation around a decision or strategy.
- An organisation wants a talk connected to practical follow-up.
