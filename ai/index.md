## AI is a tool, not an oracle

My interest in AI spans early university research, hands-on use in delivery and critical-thinking practice that keeps confident machine output from being accepted uncritically.
{: .lede}

AI can expand what one person sees and does quickly. It cannot replace judgement, domain knowledge or verification.

## The verification loop {#the-verification-loop}

I use a simple discipline: **AI expands the search space; critical-thinking tools interrogate the output; human judgement decides.** The loop repeats when better questions or new evidence emerge.
{: .lede}

In July 2025, I used ChatGPT to assemble public guidance for an unfamiliar electric hire car whose routine controls were hard to discover. It removed information friction, but I still checked safety-critical guidance rather than treating the generated answer as authoritative. My article, [Car complexity and the new user journey](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/car-complexity-new-user-journey-craig-cockburn-d7q9e), explores the experience.

## Roots in AI research

My undergraduate work in Edinburgh University's Department of Artificial Intelligence included a 1985 surface-orientation edge detector and a 1987 character-recognition project. Both addressed the enduring problem of extracting useful structure from messy input.
